Leaf Spectra Explorer

This tool lets you explore the raw leaf-level data behind Allen et al. (2026), New Phytologist: full reflectance spectra (350–2500 nm) collected as individual leaves from 13 tree and shrub species were slowly dried out on a benchtop, alongside concurrent measurements of leaf water potential and leaf water content. Pick a species and a leaf, then drag the slider (or hit play) to watch its spectrum change as it dries.
